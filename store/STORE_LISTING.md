# Chrome Web Store listing (copy-paste)

## Name
Price Softener – Gift Price Privacy

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Keep gift prices private while shopping with family on Indian stores. Checkout and payment always show the real price.

## Category
Shopping

## Language
English (India)

## Description
Buying a new phone, TV or appliance for your parents or grandparents, and they hesitate the moment they see the price?

Price Softener lets you browse together without the price getting in the way. It shows a softened, lower price on product pages, only on your own screen, so the conversation stays about what they like rather than what it costs.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose how much lower prices should look (0–60%)
• Prices are rounded to natural endings like ₹24,999 so they look real
• Small amounts such as delivery fees are left untouched
• Switch it on/off instantly with Alt+Shift+H

ALWAYS HONEST AT PAYMENT
• Checkout, address and payment pages ALWAYS show the real price. This cannot be turned off.
• The extension never changes your order, cart or the amount you pay. It only changes what is displayed on product pages in your browser.
• Optional: show real prices in the cart as well

SUPPORTED STORES
Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales, Tata CLiQ, JioMart, Myntra, AJIO, Nykaa

PRIVACY
No account, no tracking, no data collection. Your settings stay in your Chrome browser.

## Single purpose (Privacy practices tab)
Changes how product prices are displayed on supported Indian shopping sites so the user can keep gift prices private, while always showing the real price on checkout and payment pages.

## Permission justifications
- storage: saves the user's chosen percentage and on/off setting.
- Host permissions (the listed shopping sites): needed to read and change the price text shown on those pages.

## Remote code
No, this extension does not use remote code.

## Data usage
Tick nothing. The extension does not collect or transmit any user data.
Tick all three certification boxes (no selling data, no unrelated use, no creditworthiness use).

## Distribution
Visibility: Public
Regions: India only
