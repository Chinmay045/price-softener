# Privacy Policy – Price Softener

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Price Softener does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension does not read your account details, browsing history, orders or payment information.
- **What it stores.** Only your own settings (the percentage, the on/off switch and display options), saved by Chrome's built-in storage in your browser. If Chrome sync is on, Google syncs these settings across your own devices.
- **What it does on web pages.** On the supported shopping sites, it changes how prices are displayed in your browser. It never changes your cart, order or payment amount, and it does not send anything to any server.
- **No third parties.** No analytics, ads or tracking of any kind.
